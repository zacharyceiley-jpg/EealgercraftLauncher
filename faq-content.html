Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using the Eaglercraft launcher.

What is Eaglercraft?

Eaglercraft is a browser-playable project that brings Minecraft-style gameplay to a web browser. It is not an official Minecraft launcher or an official Mojang product.

How do I launch a version?

Choose an edition from the left sidebar, open the version dropdown near the bottom of the screen, select the version or client you want, and press Play.

Why is a game not loading?

Check your internet connection, wait a moment for the game to load, and try refreshing the launcher. If one version does not work, you can try another available version. Some builds may also have browser compatibility issues.

Will my worlds and settings save?

Saving depends on the specific game build and the browser's local storage support. This launcher does not guarantee cloud backups, so do not assume a world is backed up online. Keep separate backups of worlds you want to protect.

Can I play with mobile or controller controls?

Open the Mobile/Controller category in the sidebar to see the available control-focused options. Support varies by build and device.

Why are some categories empty?

Not every category has games or versions added yet. Empty categories will remain available for future additions.

Where can I read update notes?

Open the Changelog tab at the top of the launcher. It contains the version notes currently added to this launcher.

Where can I report a problem?

For launcher help, visit the launcher community Discord. For issues with official Minecraft releases, use the Minecraft bug tracker.
